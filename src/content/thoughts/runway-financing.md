A traditional pre-seed of \~250k at $5M works for development-driven companies like pure software or tech, but our next 12-months are going to require a lot more capital. For example, a CFTC / derivatives counsel engagement, registration as an NFA independent-IB (which on its own has a \~$45k net capital requirement that sits permanently on our balance sheet), a smart-contract audit before any non-testnet exposure (which of this nature are easily 5-figures), SDR reporting, subscriptions for live options & equity data, and at least one hire on the compliance side. All of that + 18 months of runway and a modest treasury for the on-chain markets we’ll have to spin up, ~$1M is roughly what would get us to being registered, audited, and marking our first trades.

I did the research, and early rounds in financial infrastructure are typically 2-5x larger than sector-neutral pre-seeds because of the licensing that needs to be underwritten. Past neobrokers & crypto brokerages have raised seven-figure seeds before their broker-dealer registrated was cleared. Even if we look at Kalshi & Polymarket who both raised significant early capital for their DCM registration process, it’s pretty clear than regulatory classification is the moat in any financial services venture, especially regarding prediction markets.

If we look at Robinhood back in October 2013, after eight months of waiting they were approved by FINRA to become a broker-dealer, and its $3M seed led by Index was raised before approval. Since the founders weren’t licensed brokers, they set up Robinhood Financial as a wholly owned subsidiary with its own management team to manage all transactions.

CFTC/derivatives counsel

$75-150k year one

3-deliverables: **the registration & perimeter memo** (fixed–fee from a boutique or former-staff practice typically low 5-figures; a major firm at partner rates would be higher), **ISDA templates drafted for an event-swap product** (Master, Schedule, CSA, confirmation, one-time build that scales across counterparties), and **registration filing support**. 

NFA independent-IB registration

~$55k parked + $15k/yr running

An independent IB must maintain adjusted net capital of at least $45k, and NFA generally expects an IB to maintain adjusted net capital of $50k so the firm does not fall below the requirement if it incurs unexpected liabilities (like defaults). This floor also scales with company headcount. 

Smart-contract audit

$60-100k

Top firms like OpenZeppelin usually charge $30-200k for enterprise engagements, while other firms quote around 50k. WIth our scope (maker, dated future, resolver adapter, plus the escrowed contracts) is a few thousand lines, so 2-3 weeks at a good firm and re-audit rounds after remediation typically adds $5-20k per pass.

SDR reporting

$10-25k/yr

Between two non-dealer ECPs, the reporting obligation falls on a counterparty, but we’ve committed to operationalizing it with LEIs per party, UTI generation, and direct SDR onboarding.

Live options & equity data

~2-5k / yr + $30k if we require Bloomberg (which is pretty likey) in the future

Compliance hire

$120k year 1

An independent IB needs a designated compliance officer and a written AML program; NFA expects internal policies and controls, a designated compliance officer, ongoing training, an independent audit, and risk-based customer due-diligence. Realistically, we’d hire an outsourced CCO firm that serves IBs rather than a full time CCO which is super expensive in NYC.
